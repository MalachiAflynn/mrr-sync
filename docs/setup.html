Setup guide

Setting up MRR Sync

From install to a contact with Stripe data on it, in about five minutes. Then what each value means and what to do when something looks wrong.

1Install the app

Open Install and press Install in HubSpot. Sign in, choose the HubSpot account to connect, read the permissions and press Connect app.

Within a minute the app adds a property group called Stripe (via MRR Sync) with nine properties on contacts and nine on companies. It never deletes or renames your own properties.

2Connect Stripe

Next you land on Connect Stripe. The app needs a restricted key, which you create in your Stripe Dashboard under Developers, then API keys, then Create restricted key. Give it exactly these permissions and leave everything else at None:

PermissionWhat for
Accounts: ReadShows your account name and default currency
Charges and Refunds: ReadLifetime paid, refunds and failed payments
Customers: ReadMatches customers to HubSpot contacts
Products: ReadPlan names
Coupons: ReadDiscounts in MRR
Invoices: ReadLast payment and failed payment dates
Prices: ReadMRR from each price
Subscriptions: ReadStatus, MRR and renewal dates
Webhook Endpoints: WriteCreates the one endpoint that tells us about changes

Paste the key and press Connect Stripe. The app tries each permission and names any that are missing. A standard secret key (it starts with sk_) is refused, because it can do far more than the app needs. After saving, only the last four characters of the key are ever shown again.

3The first sync

As soon as Stripe is connected, the app copies every Stripe customer to HubSpot, 100 at a time. The Overview shows progress as it goes, for example Backfilling 340 of 1,200 customers. From then on, changes in Stripe reach HubSpot within about a minute.

Customers without an email are skipped and noted in the sync log, since there is no safe way to match them. Add an email in Stripe and they sync on their next change.

4What each property means

All nine live in the Stripe (via MRR Sync) group, on contacts and on companies. The app manages them: a change made by hand is replaced on the next sync. Money is in your main Stripe currency and leaves out tax.

PropertyWhat it holds
Stripe customer ID
synclite_customer_id		The Stripe customer ID linked to this contact.
Stripe MRR
synclite_mrr		Monthly recurring revenue from this Stripe customer, in your main currency, before tax. Counts active and past due subscriptions.
Stripe subscription status
synclite_subscription_status		The status of this customer's Stripe subscription, or None when there is none.
Stripe plan
synclite_plan_name		The Stripe plans this Stripe customer pays for, comma separated.
Stripe next renewal date
synclite_next_renewal_date		When the next Stripe subscription renews (the earliest, for a company).
Stripe customer since
synclite_customer_since		When this Stripe customer first became a Stripe customer (the earliest, for a company).
Stripe last payment date
synclite_last_payment_date		The most recent successful Stripe payment from this Stripe customer.
Stripe last failed payment date
synclite_last_failed_payment_date		The most recent failed Stripe payment from this Stripe customer. Use it to follow up on unpaid balances.
Stripe lifetime paid
synclite_lifetime_paid		Everything this Stripe customer has paid, minus refunds, in your main currency.

MRR counts subscriptions that are active or past due. Trials, paused and canceled subscriptions add nothing. Discounts are applied, metered prices are left out, and a yearly plan counts as a twelfth of its price each month.

5Companies

A contact's primary company gets the same nine properties, worked out from every Stripe customer whose contact belongs to it:

  • MRR and lifetime paid are added up.
  • Subscription status is the most important one, in this order: active, past due, trialing, unpaid, paused, incomplete, canceled, none.
  • Plan lists every plan once, and customer ID lists every customer.
  • Next renewal date and customer since take the earliest date; last payment and last failed payment take the latest.

The app never creates companies. A contact without a company simply has no company totals, and moving a contact to another company is picked up by the daily check.

6How matching works

For each Stripe customer, the app looks for its contact in this order:

  1. The contact it saved for this customer last time, following HubSpot merges.
  2. A contact whose Stripe customer ID property holds this customer's ID.
  3. A contact with the same email, ignoring upper and lower case.
  4. Only if none match, it creates one contact with the email and the name from Stripe.

If several contacts carry the same customer ID, the oldest one is used and the sync log says so. Customers who share an email share one contact, and its values add up like a company's. On contacts that already exist, the app writes only its nine properties: email, name, lifecycle stage and owner are never touched.

7Failed payments and notes

When a payment fails, the contact's last failed payment date updates and a note titled Payment failed appears on its timeline, with the amount, the invoice and when Stripe will try again. A canceled subscription and a change of plan leave a note too. Each note is posted once, however many times Stripe sends the event.

The first sync posts no notes for the past, so a new install does not flood your timelines.

8The sync log

Sync log in the dashboard lists the newest 100 lines: what changed, for which customer, with a link to the record in HubSpot. Filter it to errors or warnings, or search by email or Stripe customer ID. Resync on a line runs that customer again right away. Resync all in Settings runs everyone, once an hour at most.

Every day at 03:00 UTC the app also checks every customer with an active or past due subscription, and anyone changed in the last two days, to catch anything a webhook missed.

9Disconnecting and uninstalling

Disconnect Stripe (Settings, Stripe, Disconnect): syncing stops, and the app removes its webhook endpoint from your Stripe account and deletes the saved key. You can connect again at any time.

Uninstall (Settings, HubSpot, Disconnect, or the connected apps in your HubSpot account settings): syncing stops right away, the Stripe endpoint and key are removed, and your plan stops renewing at the end of the period you paid for. Everything the app stores about your portal is deleted after 30 days. Installing again within those days keeps your history; you paste your Stripe key again.

Delete my data (at the bottom of Settings): after you type your portal name, everything the app stores is deleted at once, and the app is uninstalled from HubSpot.

In every case, the nine properties and the notes already written to your contacts and companies stay in HubSpot as your data. To remove them as well, delete the nine properties of the Stripe (via MRR Sync) group in HubSpot's property settings.

10Troubleshooting

Setup failed because of a property limit
Some HubSpot tiers allow only a few custom properties, and the dashboard shows what HubSpot said. Remove custom properties you no longer use, then install again from the Install page: setup runs again and only adds what is missing. Or press Contact support on the notice.
The Stripe key is missing a permission
The Connect Stripe page names each missing one. Edit the key in Stripe to add them, or make a new key, and paste it again.
It says the key is in test mode
Test keys sync test data only. Use a live restricted key for your real customers.
A customer is missing in HubSpot
Search the sync log for the customer's email or ID. Customers without an email are skipped. Fix the cause in Stripe, then press Resync on the line.
MRR looks lower than expected
Trials, paused subscriptions, metered prices, tax and amounts in other currencies are left out, and discounts are applied. The sync log flags customers with other currencies.
The dashboard asks you to reconnect HubSpot
HubSpot stopped accepting the app's access, for example after a password change or a permission change. Press Reconnect HubSpot. Syncing picks up where it stopped.
Syncing is paused
The trial ended without a plan, or a payment for the app failed more than 7 days ago. Choose or fix your plan under Billing. Nothing in HubSpot is removed while paused.
Still stuck?
Email support@example.com with your HubSpot account ID and the customer's email or Stripe ID.
