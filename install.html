Step by step

What happens when you install

  1. You sign in to HubSpot and choose the account to connect.
  2. HubSpot shows the permissions below and asks you to approve them.
  3. We add a property group called Stripe (via MRR Sync) with nine properties on contacts and companies. Nothing else in your account changes.
  4. You paste a restricted Stripe key. We check it and show exactly what it can and cannot do.
  5. We copy every Stripe customer to HubSpot, then keep them current as things change.

What we ask HubSpot for

Only what the app uses. HubSpot also adds oauth to every app, which lets you sign in to the dashboard with your HubSpot login.

PermissionWhat for
crm.objects.contacts.readFind the contact that belongs to each Stripe customer
crm.objects.contacts.writeWrite the nine properties, create a contact when none matches, and add timeline notes
crm.objects.companies.readFind each contact's primary company
crm.objects.companies.writeWrite the company totals
crm.schemas.contacts.writeCreate the property group and nine properties on contacts
crm.schemas.companies.writeCreate the property group and nine properties on companies

What we ask Stripe for, in the next step

A restricted key with these permissions and nothing else. We refuse standard secret keys, because they can do anything in your account, and MRR Sync only needs to read. The one exception is webhook endpoints: we add a single endpoint so Stripe can tell us about changes.

PermissionWhat for
Accounts: ReadShows your account name and default currency
Charges and Refunds: ReadLifetime paid, refunds and failed payments
Customers: ReadMatches customers to HubSpot contacts
Products: ReadPlan names
Coupons: ReadDiscounts in MRR
Invoices: ReadLast payment and failed payment dates
Prices: ReadMRR from each price
Subscriptions: ReadStatus, MRR and renewal dates
Webhook Endpoints: WriteCreates the one endpoint that tells us about changes

You can uninstall at any time in HubSpot, from the connected apps in your account settings. See Security for how keys and tokens are kept.
