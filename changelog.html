Changelog

What is new

What changed, newest first.

Unreleased

  • Uninstall from Settings, or from HubSpot: syncing stops, the Stripe key and webhook endpoint are removed, the plan stops renewing, and your data is deleted after 30 days unless you install again. Delete my data in Settings deletes everything at once (a minimal billing record stays for accounting) and uninstalls the app from HubSpot.
  • Old Stripe events are emptied after 30 days and deleted after 90, and the sync log keeps the newest 1,000 lines.
  • In-app billing: choose Starter ($29/month) or Growth ($59/month) with Stripe Checkout, or manage plans, update payment methods and cancel in the Customer Portal. Past due payments have a 7-day grace period before syncing pauses. Plans cap Stripe customers (500 on Starter, 5,000 on Growth); over the limit, existing customers keep updating while new ones wait for a plan upgrade.
  • The public website: a landing page, pricing, an install page that shows every permission before you connect, a setup guide, support, security, privacy, terms and data processing pages, about and this changelog. Fast, readable on phones, with no tracking and no third-party requests.
  • The dashboard: Overview with connection cards, live numbers, the latest sync run with a progress bar and notices for anything that needs attention; a searchable Sync log with links to HubSpot records and Resync; Billing; Settings with the 9 properties explained, Resync all and Delete my data. Works on phones.
  • Connecting Stripe backfills every customer to HubSpot, 100 at a time, with live counts for a progress bar. A different Stripe account replaces the old one cleanly.
  • A daily reconcile catches anything missed webhooks left out, including contacts that moved to another company in HubSpot.
  • Timeline notes on the contact for a failed payment, a canceled subscription and a plan change, each posted once however often Stripe resends the event.
  • Resync one customer by email or Stripe id, or every customer once an hour.
  • Stripe customers now sync to HubSpot: each one finds its contact (by the id we saved, our customer id, or email) or gets a new one, never a duplicate, and its 9 values are written to the contact and its primary company. Customers sharing an email or a company show combined values.
  • Syncing stays inside HubSpot's rate limits and error rate budget, waits out a 429, retries outages, and stops with one clear message when HubSpot or the Stripe key needs reconnecting.
  • npm run seed fills a Stripe sandbox with 48 sample customers for testing.
  • MRR from Stripe subscriptions, exact to the cent: every billing interval, seats, tiers, packages, coupons in the order Stripe applies them, trials, pauses and other currencies. 39 worked cases are listed in docs/mrr-cases.md.
  • Each Stripe customer becomes a snapshot of the 9 HubSpot values (MRR, status, plan, renewal, customer since, last payment, last failed payment, lifetime paid), with change detection.
  • Connect Stripe with a restricted key: every permission is checked and named when missing, standard secret keys are refused with the reason, the key is stored encrypted and only its last 4 characters are shown. The app keeps exactly one webhook endpoint in the Stripe account.
  • Signed Stripe webhooks are stored once each and queued in the same transaction; duplicates, bad signatures and unknown accounts are handled. A burst of events for one customer becomes one queued sync.
  • Database schema for tenants, connections, events, customer mapping, sync runs and log, setup, billing and audit, with tenant isolation enforced and tested.
  • Secrets are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, with key rotation.
  • Install and sign in with HubSpot. The app adds a Stripe property group with 9 properties to contacts and companies, and the dashboard shows the connection and setup status.
  • Project scaffold: workspaces, config loader, Postgres via docker compose, health check, themed web shell.
