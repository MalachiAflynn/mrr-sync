Plans and limits

About the limits

What counts as a customer?

Each Stripe customer synced to HubSpot counts once, however many subscriptions it has. Customers without an email are skipped, so they do not count.

What happens if I go over my limit?

Customers already synced keep updating. New customers wait until you move to a bigger plan, and the dashboard shows a notice. Nothing is ever deleted because of a limit.

What happens when the trial ends?

Syncing pauses until you choose a plan. Everything already in HubSpot stays where it is.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, at any time, from Billing in the dashboard. After you cancel, the plan keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

What if a payment for the app fails?

Syncing carries on for 7 days while you update your card, then pauses until the payment goes through.

Do both plans work on every HubSpot tier?

Yes. Free, Starter, Pro and Enterprise all get the same nine properties.
