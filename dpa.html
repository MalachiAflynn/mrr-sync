Data processing

Data processing terms

How we handle personal data for you when MRR Sync syncs your customers.

Draft: review before launchThis page was written from what the app does today. It has not had a legal review yet.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Scope and roles

These terms are part of our terms of service. When the app processes personal data about your customers, you decide why and how (you are the controller) and [Company legal name] processes it for you (we are the processor). They apply as long as we process that data.

Details of the processing

What and whyReading customer and billing data from your Stripe account and writing a summary of it to your HubSpot contacts and companies.
How longWhile the app is installed, then up to 30 days after you uninstall.
Whose dataYour Stripe customers, the contacts in your HubSpot account that match them, and the people in your team who sign in to the app.
Which dataNames, email addresses, Stripe and HubSpot IDs, and billing facts: MRR, subscription status, plan names, renewal, payment and failed payment dates, amounts paid and refunded, and invoice numbers.
Special categoriesNone. The app does not need or ask for them.

What we commit to

  • We process the data only to run the app for you, following your instructions: your settings in the app and these terms. If we think an instruction breaks the law, we tell you.
  • Anyone who works on the app for us is bound to keep the data confidential.
  • We keep the safeguards on our security page, including encryption of keys and tokens at rest and HTTPS in transit.
  • We help you answer requests from people about their data and give you the information you need for your own records and assessments.

If something goes wrong

If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we tell you without undue delay, with what we know, and keep you updated as we learn more.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app starts deletion: everything we hold for your account is deleted within 30 days. Delete my data in Settings deletes it right away. The data written into your own HubSpot account stays there, under your control.

Information and audits

We hold no third-party certification. We answer reasonable written questions about how we protect your data, and work with you on any review your law requires.
