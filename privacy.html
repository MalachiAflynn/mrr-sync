Privacy

Privacy policy

What the app reads, what it keeps, for how long, and who helps us run it.

Draft: review before launchThis page was written from what the app does today. It has not had a legal review yet.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Who we are

MRR Sync is run by [Company legal name]. Questions about this policy go to support@example.com.

What this covers

This website, the MRR Sync app and its dashboard. For the data about your own customers that passes through the app, we act on your behalf, under our data processing terms.

What we get from HubSpot

  • When you install or sign in: your HubSpot account ID, its domain and name, and your HubSpot user ID and email.
  • To keep records in step: contacts' emails, the nine properties the app manages, the record of merged contacts, and each contact's primary company.

The app writes its nine properties on contacts and companies, creates a contact (email and name) when a Stripe customer matches none, and adds notes about failed payments, cancellations and plan changes.

What we get from Stripe

  • Your Stripe account ID, its name, default currency and whether it is in test mode.
  • For each customer: their ID, email, name and currency.
  • Their subscriptions, prices, products, coupons, invoices and charges, to work out the nine values (MRR, status, plan, dates and lifetime paid).
  • Change notices (webhook events) Stripe sends about those customers. The app creates one webhook endpoint in your Stripe account for this and writes nothing else there.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent data, or use it for advertising.
  • Change anything in Stripe apart from the one webhook endpoint.
  • Send you marketing email. The app sends no email at all today.
  • Track you on this website: no analytics, no ad pixels, no third-party scripts.

Cookies

Two cookies, both needed for signing in, neither used for tracking:

  • sid: your dashboard session. It ends after 7 days without a visit.
  • hs_oauth_state: protects the HubSpot sign in. It lasts 10 minutes.

This website loads its fonts and images from its own address and contacts no other site.

Security

Keys and tokens are encrypted, traffic uses HTTPS, and access is kept to what the app needs. The security page has the details.

Your choices

You can disconnect Stripe, uninstall the app or delete your data at any time. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email support@example.com. If you are a customer of one of our users, contact that business first: they decide what happens to your data, and we help them.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Changes

We post any change here and update the date at the top.
