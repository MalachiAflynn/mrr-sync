What you see in HubSpot

Nine properties, kept current

Every Stripe customer is matched to one contact. Its nine values sit in their own Stripe group, on the contact and on its company, and change as Stripe does.

  • Stripe customer IDThe Stripe customer the contact is matched to.
  • Stripe MRRMonthly recurring revenue in your main currency, before tax.
  • Stripe subscription statusActive, trialing, past due, canceled and the rest.
  • Stripe planThe Stripe plans the customer pays for.
  • Stripe next renewal dateWhen the next subscription renews.
  • Stripe customer sinceWhen they first became a Stripe customer.
  • Stripe last payment dateThe most recent successful payment.
  • Stripe last failed payment dateThe most recent failed payment, to follow up on.
  • Stripe lifetime paidEverything they have paid, minus refunds.

Questions

Common questions

Something else? See the support page, or email us and a person replies within one business day.

Does it work on HubSpot Free?

Yes. It writes plain contact and company properties, which every HubSpot tier has, Free included. There are no custom objects and nothing that needs Operations Hub.

Will it create duplicate contacts?

No. It looks for the contact by the Stripe customer ID it saved, then by the customer ID property, then by email. It creates a contact only when none of those match, and customers who share an email share one contact.

Does it change anything else on my contacts?

No. It writes only its nine properties. Name, email, lifecycle stage and owner stay as they are. When it has to create a contact, it sets the email and name from Stripe.

What does it need from Stripe?

A restricted key that can read your account, customers, subscriptions, invoices, charges, prices, products and coupons, and write webhook endpoints so it can add the one endpoint that tells it about changes. Standard secret keys are refused.

How fast do changes show up?

Usually within a minute of the change in Stripe. A daily check at 03:00 UTC catches anything a webhook missed.

What happens when the trial ends?

Syncing pauses until you choose a plan. Everything already in HubSpot stays. The trial is 14 days and needs no card.

What about other currencies?

MRR and lifetime paid are in your main Stripe currency. Amounts in other currencies are left out rather than converted, and the sync log says so.

Can I choose which fields sync?

No. The nine properties are fixed on purpose: setup takes minutes, and every list and report you build works the same way in every account.
