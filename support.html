Answers

Common questions

A customer is missing or wrong in HubSpot

Open Sync log in the dashboard and search for the email or Stripe customer ID. The line says what happened. Customers without an email are skipped. After fixing the cause, press Resync on the line.

How do I change the Stripe key?

Open Settings in the dashboard and press Replace key on the Stripe card. Paste the new restricted key; it replaces the old one as soon as it checks out.

How do I uninstall?

In the dashboard, open Settings and press Disconnect on HubSpot. Or uninstall MRR Lite from the connected apps in your HubSpot account settings. Syncing stops, the app removes its webhook endpoint and key from Stripe, your plan stops renewing, and the properties already in HubSpot stay.

How do I delete my data?

Delete my data in Settings deletes everything the app holds for your account. After an uninstall it is deleted within 30 days anyway. Data in your own HubSpot and Stripe accounts stays yours.

Who can use the dashboard?

Anyone in your HubSpot account who signs in with HubSpot. There are no separate roles yet.
